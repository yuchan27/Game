Use case: stylized-concept

Style/medium: original hand-authored 2D pixel art with hard, deliberate stepped clusters and a limited dark-earth palette. At intended use, this whole square source covers 512 world pixels; compose the source so two source pixels correspond to one world pixel. Use flat color ramps and crisp clusters, not smooth rendering.
Composition/framing: square texture filled edge to edge with uninterrupted top-down ground plane; a truly seamless repeat, with every shape and color transition continuing naturally across opposite edges. Even restrained density over the whole tile, with no central focal area.
Lighting/mood: flat diffuse overcast light, dark medieval wasteland, subdued and worn.

Materials/textures: large quiet dusty earth patches with simple flat interiors; tiny grains are rare and small; hairline roots are rare and thin. No distinct rock may exceed 2% of the source width. Preserve broad calm areas between details.
Constraints: exactly one square, fully opaque PNG texture; flat top-down material only; no objects or horizon; designed to tile invisibly at a 512-world-pixel repeat size.
