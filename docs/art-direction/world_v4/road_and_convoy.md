## Confirmed root-sorting defect

The first actual atlas render hid part of the covered wagon behind the farther
depot. WorldProp enabled nested Y sorting while its sprite had a negative local
Y offset from the registered foot. The sprite's canvas top therefore entered
the scene sort independently. WorldProp now keeps its children grouped and lets
the scene sort the body foot. The same camera then showed the canopy intact and
the nearer wall in front. This follows the native
[Godot 4.6 CanvasItem contract](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/classes/class_canvasitem.html#class-canvasitem-property-y-sort-enabled).
Architecture/player ordering and collision shapes were not replaced.

## Road and event changes

Highway widths are `[8,6,6,5,6]` tiles instead of `[10,7,7,6,7]`. Short bends
and corner chamfers retain the return/spine/story/guarded-point connections.
The spine's small deviations remain subtle at gameplay scale; the route is
still a loop-and-spine layout, not a completed organic overworld redesign.

The west-turn render exposed median placement 10 across the road. Its anchor
moved from `[.225,.754]` to `[.166,.754]`, 422.5 world pixels west. Its four
physical foot rectangles remain; source geometry leaves roughly 66 pixels
between the eastern foot edge and the nearby nominal road edge. This is source
clearance plus visual evidence, not a successful traversal test.

The data validator's obsolete orthogonal-only condition conflicted with the
native capsule/polyline consumers and an existing diagonal branch. It now
requires finite normalized nonzero segments; reward reachability projects onto
the actual segment, including diagonals. Existing test contracts were updated
for zero-length rejection, diagonal rewards and grouped foot sorting. **They
were not executed.** No validation pass is claimed.

Ordinary non-story events now select optional `visual_prop` IDs in events.json.
The active kit renders these as nonblocking grounded remnants rather than large
floating inventory icons. They are spawned before the one-time ground-contact
collection. Event IDs, positions, prompts, rewards and persistence remain; the
legacy icon fallback remains when the material kit is unavailable.

Loose pickups remain recognizable RPG item glyphs, but their maximum world
extent is now 28 pixels rather than a full 64-pixel inventory icon. Native
scaling preserves source images/UI art, the 30-pixel pickup radius, item amounts
and death guards. Bobbing is one pixel without the old rotation. This keeps
small loot visually distinct from full-size environmental structures; it does
not replace inventory sources or prove pickup gameplay behavior.

## Map and notification readability

M retains all marker dots but labels exits, story/field/guarded sites and hub
NPCs/stations first. Short labels use bounded placements and avoid each other;
the closest hovered marker's full name/status is drawn in the lower legend.
Resource/enemy labels no longer cover the route with overlapping text.

HUD notices use native wrapping within the viewport and sit below the objective
and minimap panels during ordinary gameplay. Production frames cover the long
village notice; full interaction/menu/hover verification remains deferred.

## Remaining work

`captures/road-and-convoy/` owns current local render evidence. Village/Guild
still show legacy metallic tiles/neon facilities and require the next cohesive
hub art pass. Keep their IDs, service functions, approach/collision footprints
and original art while replacing their presentation. Procedural path reserves
still use expanded segment AABBs, which leave excess empty land beside long
diagonals; roadside sampling also resets per segment. Whole-route composition,
traversal and comparable performance remain open, as do hero actions/facings,
complete menus, story/progression and release checks in the production ledger.
